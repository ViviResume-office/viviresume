## About ViviResume

Choose a professional template, add your experience, and watch the page take shape in real time. Keep the draft in this browser or save it to the cloud and share it with a link.

![ViviResume](asset/viviresume-og.png)

## Create your CV in three steps

Choose a template, add your experience, then save, share, or download the result.

### 1. Choose a template

Compare layouts and choose the structure and visual style that best support your content.

### 2. Add your experience

Replace the example content, organise your sections, and refine the page while watching the live preview.

### 3. Save, share, or download

Review the finished page, then save it to the cloud, share it with a link, or download PDF, DOCX, DOC, or Markdown.

## Usage notes

Cloud saving and link sharing require sign-in. Browser drafts stay on this device.

### Flexible CV sections

Add experience, education, projects, skills, languages, certificates, a profile, and custom sections. Format the descriptions and include a profile photo or certificate images when useful.

### Detailed appearance controls

Adjust fonts, colours, type size, line height, page margins, section spacing, content scale, and automatic one-page fitting.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I keep my CV in this browser or save it to the cloud?

Yes. Your draft is saved in this browser as you edit. When you choose Save to cloud, ViviResume creates or updates the online version. Later edits stay in this browser until you save them to the cloud again.

### Where is my browser draft stored?

Your draft is saved in this browser on this device. It will not appear on another device, and clearing browser data may remove it. Save the CV to the cloud when you want an online version.

### Who can view a shared CV?

After saving your CV to the cloud, turn on sharing to create a link. Anyone with the link can view the CV. You can turn sharing off, create a new link, and choose whether visitors may download a PDF.

### Which download formats are available?

You can download your CV as PDF, DOCX, DOC, or Markdown. The PDF preserves the page layout shown in the preview; Word and Markdown keep the content editable for later use.

### Which regional language versions are available?

ViviResume provides regional interfaces, templates, and examples in 25 locales across 22 languages. Changing the site locale does not automatically translate the content you entered.

### Can I switch an existing draft to another template?

Not in the current editor. To use another template, create a new draft from that template and manually move over the content you want to keep. Your existing draft remains unchanged.
